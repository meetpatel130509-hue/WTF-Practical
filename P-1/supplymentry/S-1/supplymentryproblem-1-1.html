01 / A different kind of studio

Learn by looking closer.

We believe the most urgent design questions are also the most human ones. Our students work across disciplines and scales, from a neighborhood block to a changing climate.

With small cohorts, generous workshops, and a faculty who practice what they teach, you will find room here to make, test, listen, and begin again.
